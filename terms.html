C.W Calendar SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective: October 14, 2026

1. The program

C.W Calendar is a private text message calendar assistant operated by Cade Watkins. It sends event confirmations, updates and requested reminders to invited users who text requests to create, update or look up Google Calendar events. Participation is limited to two invited users: the account owner and one invited household member. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

2. Opting in

Invited users opt in by texting START to (714) 613-1574 from their own mobile phone. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. The full program description and disclosures are published at the SMS Program page.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your requests.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details of your plan.

5. Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to rejoin. The keywords STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT and REVOKE all stop messages.

6. Help

Reply HELP or INFO to any message for help, or email cadebrianwatkins@gmail.com.

7. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
