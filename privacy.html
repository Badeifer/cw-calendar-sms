C.W Calendar Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026

C.W Calendar is operated by Cade Watkins. This policy covers the C.W Calendar SMS program and this website.

Information collected

  • Your mobile phone number, when you opt in by texting START.
  • The content of the text messages you send and the messages the service sends back.
  • The Google Calendar event details needed to complete your requests (for example an event title, date and time).
  • Delivery records from the messaging provider, Twilio, such as when a message was sent or delivered.

How it is used

Only to process your calendar requests and to send the confirmations, updates and reminders you ask for, and to honor STOP and HELP requests and keep a record of your consent.

Service providers

Messages are delivered through Twilio. Events are stored in Google Calendar through the access you grant. Message text is processed by an AI service to interpret your requests. These providers process data only to operate this service.

SMS data sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Messaging terms

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help.

Retention

Message logs are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and are deleted on request.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to stop all messages.
  • Contact us (below) to ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.
  • Revoke the service's access to your Google Calendar at any time in your Google account's security settings.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising tags.
