C.W Calendar SMS Program

C.W Calendar is a private text message calendar assistant for invited users, operated by Cade Watkins. Invited users text requests to create, update or look up Google Calendar events and receive confirmations and the reminders they ask for. Participation is limited to two invited users: the account owner and one invited household member. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How to opt in

Invited users text START to (714) 613-1574 from their own mobile phone.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from C.W Calendar, including event confirmations, updates and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel at any time. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

After you text START you will receive this confirmation:

C.W Calendar: You are opted in to calendar confirmations and reminders. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Stopping and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. You will get one final message confirming you are unsubscribed. Reply START to rejoin.
  • Reply HELP or INFO for help, or contact cadebrianwatkins@gmail.com.
